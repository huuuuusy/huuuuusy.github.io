# ⚙️ Projects

This section lists research software, platforms, challenges, and funded projects. Research outputs are listed separately under [Publications](https://huuuuusy.github.io/#publications).

## Research Software

### Darknet-Cross: Lightweight Deep Learning Framework for Heterogeneous Computing

**2018.03 - 2018.11** · [GitHub](https://github.com/huuuuusy/Darknet-Cross)

I designed and developed a cross-platform acceleration framework for Android and Ubuntu across mobile and desktop GPUs. The project formed the engineering component of my master's thesis at HKU.

## Research Platforms

### VideoCube / MGIT Platform

**2019.11 - Present** · [Platform](http://videocube.aitestunion.com)

I built and continue to maintain the evaluation infrastructure supporting the TPAMI 2023 and NeurIPS 2023 studies. By July 2026, the platform had recorded more than 1.66 million visits, 1,900 data users, 720 registered trackers, and 960 result submissions.

### SOTVerse / VLTVerse Platform

**2021.07 - Present** · [Platform](http://metaverse.aitestunion.com)

I built and maintain the task-space evaluation platform supporting the SOTVerse research published in IJCV 2024. The platform had recorded more than 267,000 visits by July 2026.

### BioDrone Platform

**2022.05 - Present** · [Platform](http://biodrone.aitestunion.com/)

I built and maintain the benchmark platform for real-world drone tracking research published in IJCV 2024. It had recorded more than 541,000 visits by July 2026.

### GOT-10k Platform

**2020.07 - Present** · [Platform](http://got-10k.aitestunion.com/)

I maintain the evaluation platform supporting the GOT-10k research published in TPAMI 2021. By July 2026, it had recorded more than 7.56 million visits, 11,000 data users, 32,700 registered trackers, and 406,000 result submissions.

**2023.05 - 2023.11** · [Platform](http://hislopvision.aitestunion.com/)

**2021.01 - 2021.04** · [Challenge](https://celltrackingchallenge.net/)

Our method ranked second on Fluo-C2FL-MSC+ and third on Fluo-C2FL-Huh7, based on the challenge rankings recorded in October 2023.
