# About Me

<span class="intro-kicker">Research Fellow · Nanyang Technological University</span>

### Hi there, I am Shiyu Hu (胡世宇)!

I am a Research Fellow in the **[School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (SPMS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms)**, working with **[Assoc. Prof. Kang Hao Cheong](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/cris/rp/rp02319)**.

My research centers on **reliable multimodal intelligence in open and interactive environments**. I study how AI systems maintain object identity, world state, task-relevant evidence, and interaction context when observations unfold over time or humans participate. My current work spans open-world vision, multimodal reasoning, and human-centered agents.

I received my Ph.D. from the **[Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)](http://english.ia.cas.cn/)** in January 2024 under the supervision of **[Prof. Kaiqi Huang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~huangkaiqi)**, with **[Prof. Xin Zhao](https://www.xinzhaoai.com/)** serving as co-supervisor. Before that, I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the **[University of Hong Kong (HKU)](https://www.cs.hku.hk/)** with **[Prof. Choli Wang](https://www.cs.hku.hk/people/academic-staff/clwang)**.

> 📣 I welcome research discussions and collaborations. Please contact me at shiyu.hu@ntu.edu.sg.
